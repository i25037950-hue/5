SAKURA RESTAURANT — INDEPENDENT VISUAL DESIGN

This version intentionally uses a new visual identity rather than the earlier pixel-art reference.

Visual direction:
- Style: contemporary editorial luxury; spacious layouts, refined typography, full-width photography and minimal ornament.
- Palette: deep forest green #142a25, warm ivory #f5f3ed, muted sage #dce3db, antique gold #c6a16b, charcoal #202c28.
- Fonts: Playfair Display for elegant editorial headings; DM Sans for clean body text and navigation.
- Layout: full-bleed photographic hero, editorial story section, refined menu cards, experience section, dark gallery, video feature with a YouTube search link, reservation enquiry form and footer.
- Responsive: navigation wraps on small screens; grids collapse for mobile; imagery and video resize.

Target audience:
Adults, couples, families and visitors interested in contemporary fine dining and Japanese-inspired seasonal cuisine.

Purpose:
Introduce Sakura Restaurant, present sample dishes, communicate the dining concept and provide contact/reservation enquiry information.

CRAP principles:
Contrast: ivory and gold text accents stand out against forest-green backgrounds.
Repetition: consistent typography, gold accents, spacing and image treatment establish visual unity.
Alignment: sections use a shared centred content width and aligned grid columns.
Proximity: dish name, description and sample price are grouped; contact details and form fields are clearly organised.

Technical:
HTML5 and CSS3 only; internal navigation; responsive media queries; multiple images; video feature with a YouTube search link; reservation enquiry form. No JavaScript or backend is included.

Important:
Sakura Restaurant is fictional sample content. Menu items, prices, email, phone, location and opening hours are illustrative and should be replaced if real details are required. The form is front-end only and does not submit data.

Open:
Open index.html in a browser. Keep the css folder beside index.html. External images and fonts require internet access. The video feature opens YouTube search results in a new tab so you can choose a currently available fine-dining preparation video; this avoids displaying a broken or unavailable sample embed.

Publish:
Extract the ZIP, then upload the folder containing index.html and css to https://app.netlify.com/drop. Netlify will generate the public site URL after deployment.
